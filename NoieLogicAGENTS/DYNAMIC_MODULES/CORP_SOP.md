# CORP_SOP.md

## Organizational procedure integration template

This file is a template for representing a procedure supplied by an organization. It is not a universal corporate policy, authority ladder, legal interpretation, or executable workflow. Follow the relevant host policy and the organization's current approved procedure.

## Procedure record

Record the procedure owner, authoritative source and version, jurisdiction or organizational scope, effective date, required approvals, responsible roles, prerequisites, permitted actions, exceptions, escalation route, and review date. Cite the source document or system reference rather than paraphrasing critical requirements without a link.

## Decision flow

Represent each step as a condition, responsible role, permitted action, and expected evidence. A workflow may contain loops or concurrent tasks; use a DAG only if the represented dependencies are acyclic. Distinguish a process diagram from a causal model.

Before action, verify that the procedure applies to the case, required permissions and approvals are present, necessary tools are available, and material facts are supported. If a step is ambiguous or the source is stale, defer and consult the authorized owner. A generated checklist or simulation does not itself approve an action.

## Records and exceptions

Log the procedure identifier and version, decision, relevant evidence references, approvals, exceptions, and outcome according to the host's privacy and retention rules. A written template does not create audit coverage, enforce approvals, or guarantee compliance.

## Capability boundary

This Markdown repository includes no corporate workflow engine, policy registry, or approval system.
