# GOV_LAW.md

## Legal and regulatory source-review template

This file defines a source-review workflow only. It contains no jurisdiction-specific legal advice, current statute database, or compliance verifier. The applicable jurisdiction, date, entity, facts, and authoritative source must be established for each matter; obtain qualified legal review when appropriate.

## Review record

For each potentially applicable requirement, record:

- jurisdiction and competent authority;
- official source, provision, version, and effective date;
- the rule's scope, conditions, exceptions, and transition provisions;
- facts assumed and evidence still needed;
- a plain-language explanation of possible relevance;
- unresolved interpretation questions and the authorized reviewer;
- decision owner, required approval, and review date.

Do not encode legal priority as a universal SA-L tier or use a model's interpretation to override host policy or a qualified authority. A rule applies only when the source and facts support its application. If current law or jurisdiction cannot be verified, return `UNRESOLVED` and do not claim compliance.

## Capability boundary

No legal-source registry, jurisdiction resolver, legal expert system, or compliance checker is included in this repository. This template does not determine whether conduct is lawful.
