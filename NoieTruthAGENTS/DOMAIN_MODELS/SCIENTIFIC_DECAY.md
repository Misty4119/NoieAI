# SCIENTIFIC_DECAY.md

## Scientific-claim review and freshness v2.3

A scientific claim should be reviewed according to its exact scope, method, uncertainty, replication status, and date. There is no universal field-wide decay constant or single evidence ranking.

Record the result, population or system, conditions, measurement and analysis method, uncertainty, source and publication status, limitations, subsequent corrections or replications, and date checked. Distinguish mathematical derivation, measurement standard, preregistration, preprint, peer-reviewed study, systematic synthesis, and expert interpretation by their evidential role. Peer review alone does not guarantee correctness; recency alone does not invalidate older evidence.

Check whether the result was replicated independently, whether the analysis was selected after seeing data, whether multiple comparisons or publication bias matter, and whether later work changes the scope rather than directly refuting the result. Preserve minority or unresolved findings without overstating consensus.

No literature monitor or automatic scientific-consensus detector is included in this repository.
