# MEDICAL_DECAY.md

## Medical-claim freshness review v2.3

This module is a source-review checklist, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment guidance, or a numerical evidence-decay model.

For a medical claim, record source type, population, intervention or exposure, comparator, outcome, setting, publication or revision date, date checked, evidence limitations, and whether newer evidence or guidance supersedes it. Distinguish clinical trials, systematic reviews, guidelines, regulatory labeling, observational evidence, and advice for an individual patient. A study result in one population does not automatically generalize to another.

For a treatment or medication question, verify current product information and relevant safety notices for the applicable jurisdiction, and have a qualified clinician assess the individual circumstances. Do not infer present guidance from an old study, or established benefit from a recent publication alone. State if the current source could not be checked.

The FDA publishes prescribing-information resources and safety-related labeling changes; use the applicable official source for current US product information. Other jurisdictions require their own authorities. No guideline monitor, medical database, diagnostic model, or clinical validation system is included here.
