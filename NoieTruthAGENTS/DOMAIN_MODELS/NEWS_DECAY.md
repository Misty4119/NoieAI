# NEWS_DECAY.md

## News-claim freshness review v2.3

News claims can change as events develop, official records appear, or reporting is corrected. This is a review process, not a source-ranking algorithm or fixed decay model.

For a time-sensitive claim, record event time, publication and update times, named source, location, primary evidence if available, independent corroboration, and what could falsify or supersede the report. Distinguish confirmed observation, a source’s allegation, eyewitness account, analysis, forecast, and opinion. Keep a source’s statement distinct from an independently established event.

For developing events, state whether information is preliminary, disputed, or unverified and identify the next update trigger. Compare corrections and revisions rather than treating a newer timestamp as automatically more reliable. Several reports that copy one origin are one provenance path for corroboration purposes.

No live news feed, source monitor, or correction tracker is included in this repository.
