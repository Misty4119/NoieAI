# ORTHOMODULAR_LATTICE.md

## Quantum-logical formalism v2.3

An orthomodular lattice is a bounded lattice with an orthocomplement satisfying the orthomodular law. In Hilbert-space formulations of quantum logic, closed subspaces or projection operators provide a formal representation of propositions; the lattice operations and orthocomplement have model-specific meanings.

A use must identify the lattice, proposition mapping, order relation, complement, meet/join operations, state or valuation, and intended interpretation. Classical distributivity need not hold in the same way as for Boolean algebras. This formal difference does not make ordinary-language contradictions quantum, nor does a lattice result establish empirical truth.

Before using the structure for a claim set, justify how propositions map to the formal domain and how evidence, scope, negation, and uncertainty are represented. Report the exact theorem or computation and its assumptions. No orthomodular-lattice solver or general-purpose knowledge verifier is included here.
