## Source-dependence descriptor

A provenance descriptor may summarize where reports originate and which transformations or shared inputs connect them. It is a graph of recorded dependencies, not a cryptographic identity, quality score, or guarantee of independent reasoning.

Record nodes, edges, source identifiers, observation methods, transformations, time, missing links, and verification limits. Validate identity and provenance through the host's mechanisms. A shared source is a reason to avoid counting reports as independent; it does not decide the claim's truth.

No fingerprinting implementation or calibrated dependence score is included here.
