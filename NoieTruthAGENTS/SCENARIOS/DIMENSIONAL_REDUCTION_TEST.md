## Representation-reduction scenario

Design status: `DESIGN_ONLY`; no reducer or test run is included.

**Question:** Which properties of a specified mathematical representation survive a proposed projection or simplification?

Specify the source and target spaces/complexes, construction, coefficients, dimensions, maps, and property of interest. Compute only with an identified, validated implementation. Betti-number equality is a comparison of homology ranks under the chosen construction; it is not sufficient to establish homotopy equivalence, semantic fidelity, truth preservation, or absence of information loss.

Report the mathematical result and its scope separately from any interpretation about claims. Treat an unverified mapping from a knowledge representation to a topological space as a metaphor or heuristic.
## Property checklist

Before reduction, select the property whose preservation matters: exact claim text, logical consequence, provenance path, uncertainty, contradiction relation, reachability, homology, or another explicit invariant. A method can preserve one property while losing another.

Test edge cases such as duplicate nodes, disconnected components, isolated claims, parallel support paths, contradicted evidence, deleted qualifiers, and changes to filtration or coefficient field. Compare source and reduced artifacts, record the mapping and omitted data, and independently check the selected property where possible. A successful homology computation does not certify semantic or epistemic fidelity.
