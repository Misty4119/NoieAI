# EIGENSTATE_COLLAPSE.md

## Belief update and decision boundary v2.3

“Collapse” may informally describe selecting or updating among represented alternatives after evidence arrives. It does not imply a physical quantum measurement, a unique truth becoming real, or an irreversible change to external state.

For an update, retain the observation, source, prior representation, likelihood or rule, posterior or selected alternatives, and assumptions. State whether the update is reversible and what new evidence could change it. If evidence does not discriminate candidates, preserve the ambiguity. Do not confuse model selection, Bayesian conditioning, user preference, or policy choice.

An external action is a separate Logic-OS decision requiring permission and feasibility. A belief update must not trigger an irreversible action without the host decision procedure. This file contains no quantum, belief-update, or decision runtime.
