## Counterargument review

A counterargument review asks what evidence or assumptions could undermine a claim. It is a bounded review method, not a proof of robustness or an autonomous adversary.

For each important claim, list alternative explanations, potential falsifiers, data or source failures, scope limits, and assumptions that would change the conclusion. Preserve the original claim and source trail. Record what was actually checked, who or what checked it, and any unresolved concern.

A review that finds no counterexample is limited by its search scope. Do not label a claim “attack-proof,” “validated,” or true merely because it survived a checklist. No automated adversarial reviewer is included here.
