# Quantum Gravity Research Reference (Physics-OS v2.3)

**Status:** No complete and unique quantum-gravity theory has been experimentally confirmed. This document describes research directions and limits on testability; candidate frameworks must not be promoted to established laws of nature or operational capabilities.

## Scales and open problems

The Planck length, time, and energy constructed from G, ℏ, and c are dimensional scales; this does not prove that spacetime has a minimum lattice spacing, a discrete structure, or particular microscopic degrees of freedom. Classical general relativity requires a more complete theory when quantum effects or curvature scales invalidate the semiclassical approximation. The breakdown point depends on the state, background, and observable, and cannot be determined from a nominal size alone.

A candidate theory must specify its degrees of freedom, dynamics, observables, low-energy limit, background dependence, causal structure, and coupling to matter. Mathematical consistency, internal dualities, and recovery of known low-energy results are necessary research criteria, not experimental confirmation.

## Major research programs

String theory uses extended one-dimensional objects and their quantum excitations to construct candidate descriptions that include gravitational modes and gauge interactions. Worldsheet actions and critical dimensions depend on string type, background, and consistency conditions; different compactifications can yield different low-energy physics. Dualities provide important mathematical relations, but no unique vacuum has been selected by observation and the theory has not received complete experimental confirmation.

Loop quantum gravity quantizes the geometric degrees of freedom of general relativity using connection variables; spin networks and related structures provide kinematic states and spectra of geometric operators. The definition of dynamical constraints, semiclassical limit, matter coupling, and observables remain research questions. Kinematic area/volume spectra alone do not prove that spacetime is discrete in nature.

Other directions include asymptotic safety, causal dynamical triangulations, causal sets, and effective or holographic approaches, each with distinct assumptions and open problems. When comparing candidates, do not treat mathematically similar quantities as identical observable predictions.

## Holography, ER=EPR, and information claims

The holographic principle and AdS/CFT duality provide powerful mathematical frameworks and evidence within specific theories and boundary conditions; their scope is not a complete quantum-gravity description of our universe. Ryu–Takayanagi-type relations hold for specified holographic spacetimes and under approximation conditions; they are not a universal law that “all spacetime emerges from entanglement.”

ER=EPR is a conjectural program associating certain entangled black-hole states with Einstein–Rosen bridges; the original proposal focuses on black-hole systems and suggests the possibility of broader connections. It does not imply that arbitrary entangled particles are connected by traversable wormholes, communication channels, or general geometric equivalences. Information-theoretic analogies do not replace a metric, field equations, or testable quantities.

## Phenomenology and evidence standards

The Planck energy scale lies far beyond the reach of direct particle-collision experiments; candidate effects are often constrained by low-energy effective theories, astronomical observations, the cosmic background, or high-precision interferometry. In 2026, a cold-atom interferometer measured the quantum phase of freely falling wave packets and tested the equivalence principle in the low-energy quantum regime. This is an interface test of quantum matter in a gravitational background, not evidence of gravitational-field quantization or a complete quantum-gravity theory. Tabletop tests of gravity-mediated entanglement still require case-by-case examination of model assumptions and alternative explanations. Any claimed quantum-gravity signal must rule out alternatives such as instrumentation, astrophysical environments, propagation media, and ordinary general-relativistic corrections, and report model-specific predictions, systematic errors, analysis methods, and independent replication.

No direct experiment has established universal spacetime discreteness, the general ER=EPR conjecture, a specific quantum-gravity microscopic mechanism, or observation of Hawking radiation from astrophysical black holes. Label conclusions as candidate models, formal results, indirect constraints, or unknowns; absence of evidence is not disproof and must not be recorded as verified.

## Cross-domain checks

The low-energy limit must recover tested general relativity and quantum field theory, and known physics in suitable weak-field/low-energy regimes. Check dimensions, gauge/constraint consistency, unitarity or its alternative, causality, compatibility with black-hole thermodynamics, and the definition of observables. This document provides no quantum-gravity simulation, data analysis, astronomical observation, or theory-proving capability.
